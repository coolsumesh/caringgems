Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really free on Telegram?

Yes! Telegram is completely free forever. No hidden charges, no trial period. We make money from WhatsApp premium users, which allows us to offer Telegram for free.

How does family monitoring work?

You can add family members (spouse, children) who will get a notification whenever you take your medication. Great for elderly parents living alone - their children can monitor from anywhere.

Can I set multiple reminder times?

Free users get one reminder time. Premium (WhatsApp) users can set multiple reminders for different medications throughout the day.

Is my health data private?

Absolutely. We only store what's necessary - your reminder time and whether you took your medication. We never share or sell your data. Ever.

What if I miss a reminder?

We'll send a follow-up reminder after 30 minutes if you haven't responded. You can also snooze reminders if needed.

Do I need to install any app?

No! That's the beauty of CaringGems. Everything works inside WhatsApp or Telegram, which you already have. No new app to install or learn.
